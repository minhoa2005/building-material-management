Definition and Acronyms
WMSD = Warehouse Management System Development; SRS = Software Requirements Specification; UC = Use Case; FE = Feature; BF = Business Flow; SO = copied external Sales Order; POD = photo Proof of Delivery; PAY-CFM = external payment-receipt confirmation; RR = Replenishment Request; PO = Purchase Order; GR = Goods Receipt; WIR = Warehouse Issue Request; OD = Outbound Delivery; PGI = Post Goods Issue; TR = Transfer Request; TD = Transfer Dispatch; MWTR = destination transfer receipt; ADJ = actual nonzero stocktake quantity adjustment; FD/GR-FD = failed-delivery case/actual return receipt; RECLASS = total-preserving quality/location reclassification at B1 closure; UOM = unit catalogue entry (code, name, carton flag, optional area mm²); Conversion = versioned base-unit factor per product and optional lot; Document ID = [document type]-[warehouse or department code]-[YYMMDD]-[sequence], stored as the document _id.
Warehouse Staff is the canonical existing warehouse application role. Put-away/picking tasks may retain TO as a historical task abbreviation; no redundant inter-warehouse TO layer. Custody is physically held customer/supplier-owned stock. Transit is a stock-account state, not a quality grade. Available stock additionally meets verified identity, GOOD quality, eligible location, ownership, freeze and reservation conditions. N–X–T means Inbound–Outbound–Closing quantity report.

I. Overall Requirements
1. Context Diagram
Five default roles, plus custom roles that the Administrator builds only from the fixed permission catalogue, with exactly one role assigned to each user: Sales copies verified external SO, manages linked WIR/OD and customer-return demand and confirms full external payment receipt (UC-130); assigned Warehouse records physical operations and photo POD (UC-129); Accountant agrees PO terms with the supplier outside WMSD, then records, issues and reschedules the PO and requests/executes remainder closure, and records supplier-return agreements/ACK and external accounting references; Owner makes scoped decisions, clears differences and maintains products, UOM/conversions and the warehouse/location catalogue; Administrator manages accounts (lock with reason), custom roles and permissions without business-posting rights.
MISA, Customer, Supplier and Carrier are staff-mediated business parties/systems, not direct API/login actors. Commercial pricing policy, invoices/refunds, payment execution, debt and accounting remain external; WMSD keeps the copied SO unit selling price only for permitted roles. WMSD SO is a manually copied source record; POD/PAY-CFM track fulfilment without executing payment. No automatic channel/MISA integration. D2/D3 retain personal departmental entry and five roles despite survey-reported shared entry/delegation.
API-01 SMTP sends post-commit warehouse notifications. API-02 controlled storage supports private evidence upload/retrieval; the implementation stores authenticated Cloudinary assets and issues short-lived signed download URLs only after document/warehouse scope checks.

2. Main Business Flow
The following eight business flows define actors, source documents, alternate outcomes and inventory effects. Operational demand, physical execution, approval and external financial confirmation remain distinct.

2.1 BF-01 — Replenishment, purchasing and goods receipt

Trigger: a saved SO has uncovered quantity after checking compatible timely sources and existing requests; or availability remains below its warehouse minimum after committed stocktake closure/unlock; or Sales/Warehouse records another reasoned need.

Sales Staff or Warehouse Staff may also open Create replenishment request directly from the menu for a reasoned other need, without a sales shortage, a low-stock alert or a stocktake result. An RR may consist entirely of MANUAL_OTHER lines. For each such line, select a product and enter a positive quantity and unit. The request requires a receiving warehouse, a need date and a reason at the request level. Line reasonNote is optional (up to 2,000 characters). Whenever the server evaluation raises a warning (OPEN_REQUEST, INCOMING_PO or STOCK_SUFFICIENT), the creator must confirm the independent need and enter an explanation before Save draft/Submit. Review current stock, existing requests and incoming supply to avoid duplicate purchasing. Availability at or above the minimum does not by itself block this path. Do not invent SO/stocktake references or source-specific evaluation snapshots for MANUAL_OTHER. Submit to a different Store Owner for approval; Accountant creates the PO only after approval. RR creation and approval do not change stock.

**Main flow:**

1. For each line the system evaluates, for the same receiving warehouse and product: available GOOD/STORE stock in pickable STORAGE (net of reservations, open tasks, defect holds and frozen locations), the warehouse minimum/target, open RR quantity (DRAFT, SUBMITTED or approved but not yet ordered), outstanding OPEN/PARTIAL PO quantity with its common expected date, and other-warehouse availability within the user's scope. The result is advisory: OPEN_REQUEST, INCOMING_PO and STOCK_SUFFICIENT warnings never block on their own. Line sources are MANUAL_OTHER (default), STOCK_THRESHOLD, SALES_SHORTAGE (saved SO or WIR line) or STOCKTAKE_LOW_STOCK (closed stocktake); SO/WIR and stocktake references are kept for traceability. A SO source does not require WIR first.
2. Create request opens one form. One RR has one receiving warehouse and need date. Units come from the active UOM catalogue and the current product/lot conversion; a carton unit requires a verified lot. The creator decides the requested quantity. Opening/cancelling persists no RR. On Save draft/Submit the server re-evaluates every line inside the transaction; a warned line without independentNeedConfirmed and a reason is rejected while entered fields are preserved for review. A saved source-backed line cannot change its source document; remove it and add a new line instead.
3. Save draft records DRAFT; Submit records the actual sender, current version and per-line warning snapshot as SUBMITTED. A different Owner approves each line in base units within the requested quantity, rejects or returns with reasons. The creator may withdraw a DRAFT/SUBMITTED RR; the Owner cancels an approved RR that has no PO. MANUAL_OTHER retains its business reason without invented source evidence; approval alone has no stock effect and no PO is created before approval.
4. Accountant agrees quantity, price and delivery date with the supplier outside WMSD, then creates the PO from the APPROVED RR. One RR has at most one PO, for one supplier and the RR receiving warehouse; each RR line appears at most once and its base quantity cannot exceed the approved quantity. The PO is saved as DRAFT, edited only while DRAFT and issued as OPEN with one common expected date. While OPEN/PARTIAL only that expected date can be rescheduled with a reason; supplier confirmations and PO amendments are not recorded in WMSD.
5. For every delivery, Warehouse records one goods arrival with its inspection: arrival time, deliverer and delivery reference; per PO line the actual verified lot (a PO line delivered in several lots is split here), the counted quantity in carton or piece units converted with the lot conversion, quality, packaging SEALED/OPENED/TORN/UNKNOWN and ownership decision ACCEPT/CUSTODY/REJECT. Allowance is checked in base units. A PO may have multiple arrivals. Q8 permits Owner/Accountant co-inspection recorded in evidence; GR posting stays with Warehouse. Torn packaging alone is not a tile defect; opened cartons require actual piece counts.
6. Split quantities into accepted good, pending inspection/agreement, defective, or rejected/immediately returned. Record short/over/wrong-product/wrong-lot evidence.
7. Confirming the inspection creates a draft purchase GR for ACCEPT quantities and a draft custody receipt for CUSTODY quantities; neither changes stock. Posting the GR increases STORE-owned stock once at the actual RECEIVING/quarantine location after checking PO allowance, quality, capacity and freeze; opened or loose goods require measured packaging portions. The custody receipt posts SUPPLIER-owned stock to a CUSTODY/QUARANTINE location and never increases PO received quantity; Accountant later decides ACCEPT (ownership transfer without a second physical receipt) or RETURN (custody release). Owned defective goods enter BF-02; immediately rejected goods must not be fictitiously received then issued.
8. Warehouse Staff creates put-away tasks from the posted GR within its unplanned quantity and confirms actual placed quantity/location; partial confirmation keeps the remainder, which may be cancelled. Each put-away line moves whole cartons, optionally plus the entire loose remainder; cartons are never opened automatically and the split is validated when the task is created. Only accepted store-owned goods meeting quality/location checks become allocatable. Custody has separate records/holding positions; rejected goods are handed back, not processed as accepted GR/put-away stock.
9. The PO becomes PARTIAL after its first posted GR and COMPLETED when fully received. To stop waiting for an unreceived remainder, Accountant requests closure, a different Owner approves it and Accountant executes it; the PO becomes CLOSED and the cancelled quantity is stored separately from the ordered quantity. A wrongly posted purchase GR is reversed directly by Warehouse with evidence when no dependent operation exists; a CLOSED PO stays CLOSED. Warehouse completion need not await accounting.

Exceptions: shortage leaves remainder open; over-delivery stays separate pending acceptance; a different manufacturer lot is not substituted merely because SKU names match; zero accepted quantity still has a discrepancy case; return of previously received goods uses BF-08; replacements are new linked receipts.

Inventory effect: RR/approval/PO do not increase stock. Posted receipt increases the correct stock category. Put-away changes location/allocation readiness, not total a second time.

Documents: RR, PO, arrival/inspection, GR, put-away task and discrepancy record. Reference: SAP WM I pp. 10–11, 18–22 and [MISA purchase receipt](https://helpeshop.misa.vn/kb/huong-dan-su-dung-tinh-nang-nhap-kho-tren-phan-mem-misa-eshop). Lot identity (product + manufacturer lot with fixed pieces per carton) and permissions are WMSD-specific controls.

2.2 BF-02 — Defect detection, isolation and disposition

**Trigger:** cracks/quality defects discovered in BF-01/03/04/05/06/07 or routine work.

1. Warehouse records source, lot, location, quantity, symptoms, time and photos/record.
2. Immediately record allocation blocking and a quarantine case without awaiting Owner approval. In B1 frozen stocktake scope, initially record defect hold/evidence only; quality/location changes use the approved BF-05 closure batch, never ordinary posting to bypass the freeze. Retain blocking until reclassification/quarantine completes. Replan affected reservations and notify Sales.
3. Verify condition and ownership/custody. Quarantine alone never transfers ownership.
4. Warehouse proposes release after reinspection, regrade, supplier return or destruction. Supplier return follows BF-08 with Accountant-recorded agreement, without Owner dispatch approval; destruction/regrade/release outside stocktake still uses step 5 Owner authorization. Discounted sales remain external and do not turn defects into good stock.
5. For destruction/regrade/release outside stocktake, Owner approves precise action/quantity or requests evidence; otherwise retain isolation. Supplier return bypasses this approval. B1 stocktake reclassification uses UC-104 approval and UC-105 closure rather than duplicate UC-77 approval.
6. Warehouse confirms authorized destruction/regrade/release; destruction requires actual evidence/quantity. Supplier return goes to BF-08 on Accountant/supplier agreement without Owner approval. In B1 quality/location posts only through stocktake closure, never ordinary UC-78.
7. Post exactly once. Close only when every quantity is resolved or linked to an accountable open case.

Inventory effect: quarantine/regrade preserves store-owned totals. Destruction reduces actual destroyed quantity; supplier return deducts in BF-08, not again here. Previously deducted quantities cannot be deducted through another case.

Documents: defect case, inspection, disposition decision, destruction record/return issue. These controls address tiles and panel feedback; the four SAP cases do not provide this complete workflow to copy.

2.3 BF-03 — Same-warehouse relocation

Trigger: goods must move between locations within one physical warehouse. Warehouse Staff executes without Owner approval or an inter-warehouse Transfer Request (TR).

1. Select the warehouse and source location, then individual balances (separate product/lot/quality/owner rows, searchable by name, SKU or lot) or the whole location, enter a positive whole number of cartons/pieces, one destination location and a reason. Check both locations belong to the same warehouse and differ, the lot is verified, the source quantity is sufficient after other open tasks and no stocktake lock applies. The server simulates the packaging split: sealed cartons stay whole and loose/open portions move whole; cartons are never opened automatically.
2. Create a relocation task (TO-RELOCATE) in OPEN state. Only its creator may edit or cancel it while OPEN. Preserve reservations and demand links for already committed goods.
3. The creator confirms TO-RELOCATE once for the whole order: every line must be submitted and each actual quantity must equal the planned quantity. There is no partial confirmation; to change quantity or destination, edit the OPEN order first or cancel it. Physical movement/counting supports confirmation rather than a standalone software activity.
4. Atomically decrease source/increase destination by the same quantity, preserving warehouse, owner, lot and quality and checking destination capacity. Move the reservation with the stock and retain audit history; warehouse total is unchanged.

End: the task is CONFIRMED in full or CANCELLED before posting; a confirmed relocation is immutable. A physical move to another warehouse requires a separate BF-04 case.

Documents: TO-RELOCATE and location confirmation history. This process uses UC-47–50, without TR, TD or MWTR.

2.4 BF-04 — Inter-warehouse transfer and discrepancy resolution

Trigger: stock must move between two physical warehouses for balancing or fulfilment. Warehouse requests, Owner approves, and source/destination confirm independently. Same-warehouse relocation uses BF-03 instead.

1. Check compatible availability at configured warehouses (Sales sees only the warehouse catalogue, not operational balances). Direct dispatch from another warehouse uses BF-06 there, without a fictitious receipt at the main warehouse. Warehouse Staff assigned to the source warehouse creates a TR with source and destination (the destination may be outside the creator's assignments), products, units, quantities and reason; it is saved as PENDING_APPROVAL.
2. A different Owner approves, rejects or returns the TR (REVISE → REVISION_REQUIRED). The creator edits and resubmits a returned TR, preserving line IDs. Approval does not reserve stock. Do not add another inter-warehouse order or Owner approval of TO; picking/put-away TOs are internal tasks with execution confirmation.
3. Source Warehouse creates a draft Transfer Dispatch (TD) from approved TR lines, choosing GOOD/STORE balances with verified lots and a source STAGING location. The draft reserves the source quantity at once and cannot exceed the approved remainder; one TR may have several TDs. Cancelling a draft TD releases its reservation, open requirement/task and TR allowance without stock posting.
4. From the TD, Warehouse generates exactly one internal requirement and one PICK TO; the reservation moves to the task. The creator confirms the PICK together with the handover (recipient name, optional vehicle plate): one atomic posting moves source → staging → transit and sets the TD to POSTED. TD is the transfer goods issue; do not post an additional GI for the same quantity.
5. A destination user different from the dispatcher receives against the posted TD, in one or several receipts (GoodsReceipt TRANSFER): GOOD quantities into a RECEIVING location, DAMAGED quantities into QUARANTINE with a damage reason. Each receipt reduces transit by the actual quantity; unreceived quantity stays in transit and receipts cannot exceed it. Preserve demand allocation throughout source → transit → destination.
6. For GOOD receipt lines Warehouse generates one PUTAWAY requirement/TO and confirms receiving → STORAGE without a second receipt increase; an all-DAMAGED receipt needs no TO. Unreceived quantity is reported as a transfer difference record at the destination; a different Owner approves it against a later posted receipt of the same TD, or rejects/returns it, keeping it open. A matched release does not automatically complete the entire TR.
7. Close the TR only when every approved quantity is dispatched, transit is zero, every required GOOD put-away is complete and no difference record is open. When the remaining approved quantity will not be dispatched, a partial close stops further TDs after everything already dispatched has been received and put away. Posted quantities are never edited.

Example: A dispatches 100 boxes; B receives 98: 95 good + 3 broken. B receives 95 good and 3 quarantined; 2 remain transit/unresolved. Do not leave all 100 in transit or treat the 3 physically present damaged boxes as lost. The 2 boxes stay in transit under an open difference record until a later posted receipt is approved; the transfer function does not write them off automatically.

**Inventory effect:** store-managed total including transit is preserved by ordinary transfer; only valid loss/destruction/external issue reduces it.

Documents: TR, TD, handover, MWTR, discrepancy case. SAP WM II distinguishes STO, issue/receipt and put-away TO. [MISA transfer](https://helpeshop.misa.vn/kb/huong-dan-cach-chuyen-kho-tren-phan-mem-misa-eshop) distinguishes warehouse moves within a branch and inter-branch transfers. WMSD applies physical warehouses of one store and review1's two-document requirement without inferring multiple branches.

2.5 BF-05 — Stocktake, reconciliation and adjustment

**Trigger:** monthly periodic stocktake per Q14, area counts or suspected discrepancies.

1. Owner plans scope by warehouse/location/lot/status, schedule and counters. Adjustment requester cannot approve their own request.
2. Resolve unfinished receipt/issue/transfer tasks in scope. Separate staged, quarantined, custody and transit stock; never count both ends of a transfer.
3. Activate an **immutable cutoff snapshot** and export/print a **count sheet** by location/lot/status. Allow newly found items. Prefer blind count sheets without book quantities.
4. Selected MVP: lock quantity/location/status-changing transactions in the counted scope until the session is resolved. Other areas continue. This replaces the earlier Report 3 post-cutoff reconciliation model. Do not implement partial locking without reconciliation.
5. Warehouse enters physical carton/piece counts, condition, user and time. Blank means uncounted, not zero. Each Enter Recount creates a retained count attempt rather than overwriting a previous one.
6. Compare complete/valid counts with snapshot across quantity, location and quality. Variance with insufficient evidence uses Start Recount → Enter Recount → compare again, repeatable; sufficient evidence proceeds to explanation/resolution in step 7 without mandatory recount. Present damaged goods are not automatically missing quantity. Under B1 immediately record defect hold/evidence without ordinary balance/quality/location posting in the frozen scope.
7. No quantity/location/quality variance and no unresolved defect hold → close/unlock without ADJ=0. Otherwise Warehouse proposes a versioned plan: actual shortage/overage delta, total-preserving RECLASS and quarantine location with execution evidence. Book 50 good / count 48 good + 2 broken means total delta=0 and RECLASS2, never shortage -2 or ADJ=0. Unsubstantiated portions retain freeze/recount.
8. Owner approves/returns the plan through UC-104. Warehouse records actual quarantine/placement confirmation against that plan under controlled session closure; Owner performs UC-105 to post nonzero delta if any and RECLASS/location movements in the same authorized, idempotent ledger batch. Retain freeze until every line, defect hold and quarantine task is resolved; failure rolls back the entire batch without partial unlocking. Quality-only resolution still needs a decision/RECLASS but no ADJ=0. Owner cannot approve a self-originated plan.

Feasibility: D5 selected locking; the following advanced alternative is not selected. Count small areas with Owner available to avoid prolonged locks. If operations must continue during counting, expected quantity at count time = snapshot + receipts after cutoff − issues after cutoff; delta = physical count − expected quantity. Apply delta to current stock, never replace current stock with an old count. This requires per-line count timestamps and late/backdated movement controls. These are distinct designs and cannot be mixed casually.

Documents: session, count sheet, counts/recounts, variance record, adjustment request, approval and increase/decrease documents. SAP WM IV pp. 2, 4–6, 12–17, 20–21, 26–27 supports freeze/recount/clearing concepts, not a requirement to implement SAP WM/MM dual books or value accounting. [MISA stocktake](https://helpeshop.misa.vn/kb/huong-dan-cach-kiem-ke-kho-tren-phan-mem-misa-eshop) informs quantity/reason/document handling; immutable snapshot and approval controls are WMSD To-be.

2.6 BF-06 — Sales order, warehouse issue, customer delivery and completion

Trigger: Sales Staff receives a verified external order from MISA, paper or another source. Sales records the order in WMSD before requesting warehouse issue. Store pickup and site delivery follow the same process.

1. Sales creates a Sales Order (SO) with customer, need date, delivery address, source system (MISA/PAPER), reference number/date, an uploaded source image, and lines with source line key, product, optional lot, unit, quantity, unit selling price and currency. Keep original order/line references and source evidence. Verify phone/Zalo instructions and record a source order before entry; do not duplicate the same external order. Entry is manual, without live MISA synchronization.
2. The SO creator creates a Warehouse Issue Request (WIR) from the saved SO with warehouse, need date and base quantities, linked to that order and its individual lines; WIR changes no stock. One SO may have several WIRs; each WIR may have several outbound releases (OD), with one warehouse per release.
3. Check net SO/WIR/OD outstanding fulfilment, commitments/reservations and compatible availability per line. Net = valid PGI less posted GR-FD; absent goods do not reopen demand. Shortages use other supply/BF-04 or BF-01, without negative stock or duplicate demand.
4. Sales agrees full delivery, partial delivery or waiting with the customer. Record customer consent before substituting a lot.
5. Sales creates a warehouse-specific OD for agreed WIR demand with planned date, recipient name/phone and delivery address, choosing eligible GOOD/STORE balances with verified lots in pickable STORAGE. The OD is RELEASED and reserves the quantity atomically after version, freeze and availability checks. Warehouse then creates the picking task from the OD reservations: the system creates a draft GI-S and a PICK TO to a STAGING location and moves the reservation to the task; FIFO applies within compatible supply. Draft GI does not reduce stock.
6. The assigned Warehouse user enters actual picked quantities (cartons/loose pieces) and confirms the picking TO, possibly in several partial confirmations, moving storage → STAGING with the reservations; the task becomes PARTIAL then CONFIRMED and the GI-S becomes PICKED, not POSTED. Physical picking/counting supplies the execution evidence; TO confirmation only changes location, not inventory total.
7. On shortage or damage, Warehouse records evidence, blocks suspect stock and informs Sales. Defects use BF-02; unexplained book/physical differences use BF-05. Never overwrite the balance directly.
8. Warehouse updates draft GI-S to confirmed actual picking; retries reuse document/operation key rather than creating another issue. Distinct valid handovers may have separate GI-S within the OD's remaining fulfilment quantity. At actual handover record recipient, time, HO and PGI the actual quantity once. Before handover GI remains DRAFT and stock stays staged or is traceably unpicked. Carrier handover does not prove customer receipt.
9. Warehouse records actual customer POD with recipient, time, quantity per posted GI-S in the same SO/WIR/OD and required photo. Enforce shared POD/GR-FD/open-FD cap below; no POD for FD quantity. Missing photo/awaiting receipt updates only POD without repeated picking/PGI. Partial delivery → PARTIALLY_DELIVERED; full active quantity > 0 with photos → DELIVERED.
10. After SO is DELIVERED, Sales Staff confirms full money receipt outside WMSD. Keep confirmer, time and receipt reference, evidence or confirmation note (PAY-CFM). Both delivery and external receipt confirmation are required for COMPLETED. Unpaid/partial/unclear payment leaves DELIVERED; prepayment cannot complete an undelivered order.

Quantity and status controls:

| Subject | Rule |
|---|---|
| SO → WIR | WIR commitments include net fulfilment and remaining demand, capped by SO after valid cancellation. GR-FD reopens the same WIR source quantity without duplicate demand. |
| WIR → OD → GI-S | OD commitments remain within WIR; valid PGI less posted GR-FD cannot exceed authorized fulfilment. Replacement OD transfers reopened commitment without double-counting; retain gross dispatch history. |
| GI-S → POD | Per GI line, effective POD + posted GR-FD + open FD claims cannot exceed valid unreversed PGI, checked atomically. FD-received/claimed quantity cannot receive POD; same-key retries return stored result. |
| Remainder cancellation | Sales cancels only unissued or received-back GR-FD quantity not redelivered, resolving tasks/reservations/version. No cancellation of goods not yet returned. Full cancellation at zero delivery → CANCELLED; partial delivery plus valid remainder cancellation permits DELIVERED only for active quantity > 0 with full POD. |
| Warehouse fulfilment versus order completion | WIR may close on full issue. SO still requires photo-confirmed customer delivery followed by Sales confirmation of full external receipt. |

After PGI, failed-delivery goods returning use BF-07-FD (A1): posted GI-S + failure evidence → inspection → GR-FD. No fictitious POD or ordinary D6 return eligibility is required. Posted GR-FD reopens exactly its net-fulfilment quantity on the original SO/WIR/OD; Sales chooses waiting/redelivery/cancellation of that reopened portion. Status edits cannot restore stock or rewrite issues. Mistaken POD/PAY-CFM is traceably voided with reason/history and recalculated progress. Ordinary post-delivery CUSTOMER_RETURN uses D6, preserves original POD/PAY-CFM and does not automatically reopen sales demand.
**Net fulfilment:** cumulative valid PGI less posted GR-FD for the same source lines. SO/WIR/OD execution caps use net fulfilment and remaining commitments, not gross physical dispatch without deducting accepted failed-delivery receipts. Redelivery uses reopened demand on the same SO/WIR; reuse an eligible OD or transfer only the reopened commitment to a linked/versioned replacement OD, without duplicate demand. Preserve all gross PGI/GR-FD ledger events. Cancel only unissued demand or received-back GR-FD quantity not yet redelivered; unresolved off-site goods cannot be cancelled to bypass controls.

Inventory effect: SO/WIR creation changes no stock; the reservation made at OD release reduces availability; picking changes location; PGI deducts once. POD and PAY-CFM only update progress, without stock postings or payment execution.

Documents: SO, WIR, OD, picking TO, GI-S/HO, photo-evidenced POD and PAY-CFM. SAP WM III p. 2, pp. 12–17 and pp. 21–22 inform picking and PGI, not WMSD payment scope. [MISA sales issue](https://helpsme.misa.vn/2022/kb/xuat_kho_ban_hang/) informs requests, warehouse documents and responsibilities.

2.7 BF-07 — Post-delivery customer returns and failed-delivery intake

**Select the source branch:** CUSTOMER_RETURN for goods actually received by the customer; FAILED_DELIVERY (BF-07-FD) for posted issues not confirmed by POD that physically return. Neither branch inherits the other's eligibility or ownership assumptions.

#### 07.CR — Return after customer receipt

D6: sealed boxes/unused loose pieces are assessed individually. Installed/customer-damaged/verified-overdue goods are ineligible; Owner cannot override these exclusions. Do not invent a return period; unverified conditions/deadlines remain PENDING. Supplier defects may be reviewed as exceptions, distinct from customer-caused damage.

1. Sales creates CR from POD/GI lines or Accountant-verified external history, recording reason/quantity without fictitious historical GI/POD.
2. Per source line, posted ordinary receipts plus open claims cannot exceed eligible actual customer-received quantity. Verified externally delivered quantity substitutes for internal POD only for historical sources. Unknown origin waits for verification.
3. Warehouse records customer custody pending inspection, without owned/available increase; enters actual lot, quantity, packaging, use/installation, damage-cause and deadline evidence.
4. **C2 — Ordinary case:** verified source/quantity, sealed or unused loose tiles, good condition, verified return terms/deadline and no dispute/deviation. After inspection Sales Staff accepts/rejects each quantity and may be the CR creator. This is explicitly selected ordinary commercial acceptance authority, not self-granted Owner approval or automatic acceptance of sealed goods.
5. **Exception:** supplier defects, disputes or agreement deviations that are not D6 exclusions go to Owner after inspection/evidence, with a different user from the exception proposer. Unknown eligibility stays PENDING; approval cannot replace verification. No Owner approval for ordinary cases and no repeated approval after GR.
6. Warehouse posts GR-CR only for the current inspected/eligible version accepted by Sales or exception-approved by Owner: good goods use confirmed put-away TO before availability; accepted defects enter quarantine/BF-02/08. Rejected goods remain custody until actual handback. Commercial acceptance itself posts no stock. GR-CR posting/rejection/closure releases the exact CR claim so resolved quantity is not held twice.
7. Sales communicates; Accountant records external invoice/refund references. Close warehouse work once every quantity is received/handed back or assigned to an accountable open case, independently of finance. Ordinary returns preserve original POD/PAY-CFM and do not automatically reopen sales demand.

Example: 10 returned boxes: Sales accepts 7 good, Owner approves 2 supplier-defective as exceptions, and 1 wrong-source box is rejected → +7 good after put-away, +2 quarantine, custody1 until handback.

#### 07.FD — A1: Failed-delivery receipt

1. Warehouse creates FD from POSTED GI-S and the same SO/WIR/OD lines, failure/handover evidence and expected returning quantity. No commercial CR or Owner approval is required; no fictitious POD or D6 test for goods the customer never received.
2. Atomically claim/check each GI line: effective POD + posted GR-FD + open FD claimed quantity cannot exceed valid unreversed PGI. POD-confirmed quantity cannot also use FD; customer-received goods use 07.CR.
3. Warehouse records ARR/INS with actual returned quantity, lot, quality and dispatch-trip evidence. Track receipt against the issue, not customer-owned custody. Only verified quantities may post; wrong-source/unresolved goods remain separately held/unposted, outside owned/available balances.
4. Warehouse posts GR-FD exactly once using UC-86, UC-87 failed-delivery mode: increase actual received quantity by quality; GOOD awaits put-away TO, DAMAGED goes to quarantine/BF-02/08. Never edit the original GI/PGI or post GR-CR for the same quantity. Atomically convert received FD claim into GR-FD and release the corresponding open claim; never count both for one quantity. Cancelling an unposted claim only releases its hold, without stock/net-fulfilment changes.
5. In the GR-FD commit, update net fulfilment = valid PGI − posted GR-FD, reopen exactly linked SO/WIR/OD demand, increment version and recalculate status. Sales chooses wait/redeliver/cancel received-back demand without duplicate SO/WIR. Redelivery has new GI/POD within net caps; any replacement OD transfers the reopened commitment with traceable linkage rather than adding demand.
6. Close FD only when every claimed quantity has evidenced receipt/correction/non-return outcome. Goods not physically received cannot increase stock or reduce executed fulfilment. SO becomes DELIVERED only for a fully POD-confirmed active obligation greater than zero; total cancellation with zero actual delivery means CANCELLED, never COMPLETED. Old PAY-CFM authority does not carry over to a changed version.

2.8 BF-08 — Supplier return and quantity reconciliation

**Trigger:** wrong/defective goods or other supplier-agreed returns from BF-01/02/07 or existing stock.

1. Warehouse proposes return linked to supplier, source PO/receipt, lot/location, quantity, reason and evidence. Defects are immediately blocked; good goods proposed for return are reserved for the case.
2. Accountant verifies documents and records supplier agreement/refusal, quantity, schedule, handover method and references. Q20 also permits Owner/purchasing personnel to contact suppliers externally; Accountant still records WMS evidence without a purchasing role. Supplier has no WMSD login.
3. Accountant verifies supplier agreement against source/lines/quantity before dispatch; Warehouse checks case quantity and duplicate handling. BF-08 requires no Owner dispatch, variance or closure approval. A historical BF-02 decision is a reference if present, never a mandatory dispatch prerequisite.
4. Warehouse creates draft GI-SR against source and supplier-agreed quantity, records correct-lot picking and confirms actual handover. Missing agreement or unclear source/quantity keeps the case open without dispatch; supplier refusal does not make defective goods available.
5. Record actual handover/issue. Store-owned stock deducts once; goods rejected at arrival and held for supplier custody only reduce custody.
6. Accountant records supplier receipt evidence; Warehouse provides transport evidence. Compare dispatched versus acknowledged quantity. Short/damaged/partially refused amounts remain open; do not rewrite dispatch to the supplier's desired quantity.
7. Accountant reconciles and records supplier-agreed resolution per quantity; Warehouse confirms supporting physical documents. Unresolved disputes remain open without a BF-08 Owner approval step. Goods coming back increase stock only after linked receipt/inspection; compensation does not recreate quantities. Destruction/release of defective stock still on site uses BF-02 authorization.
8. Accountant records Invoice/Credit Note or applicable external references through UC-122, UC-123: type, ID/date, purchase/GI-SR lines and reconciled quantity, evidence/verification state. ,  messages do not prove debt adjustment; unverified finance remains PENDING_EXTERNAL and no Credit Note is required before dispatch. Accountant closes warehouse work through UC-92 when every quantity reconciles to Warehouse-confirmed documents, without Owner approval; finance remains independent and closure makes no further deduction.

BF-08 inventory effect: SR/agreement verification does not reduce total; actual Warehouse dispatch reduces source-correct owned/custody stock once. ACK/closure never deducts again. Replacement requires actual new receipt, never stock from agreement/money.

BF-08 documents: SR, Accountant-verified supplier agreement, GI-SR or CUS-OUT/HO, ACK/VAR, reconciliation outcome and EXT-REF. No Owner approval. Section 13 MISA purchased-goods-return guidance is reference only, not a WMSD approval/accounting requirement.

3. Conceptual Data Model
3.1 Entity Relationship Diagram
Each user, including inactive accounts, must have exactly one role: one of the five default roles OWNER/SALES/WAREHOUSE/ACCOUNTANT/ADMIN or an Administrator-created custom role; many users may share a role. Permissions come only from the current role, with no combined roles or session role switching. Assigned warehouses remain independent and may include multiple warehouses. Role changes affect subsequent requests and never rewrite historical actors or decisions.
The three Mermaid ERDs below show logical views for A1, B1 and C2/BF-08, including key PK/FK/attributes and selected relationships. These are logical views. The implementation uses 58 MongoDB collections: one collection per business document, embedded items[] with stable _id and source links, no generic document/document_line persistence, and five GoodsReceipt kinds sharing goods_receipts.

4. User Requirements
4.1 Actors
Five default application roles operate within their assigned permissions and record/warehouse scope. The Administrator may add custom roles by selecting existing permissions; a custom role is evaluated by its permissions, never by its name. Each user, including inactive accounts, must have exactly one role: one of the five default roles OWNER/SALES/WAREHOUSE/ACCOUNTANT/ADMIN or an Administrator-created custom role; many users may share a role. Permissions come only from the current role, with no combined roles or session role switching. Assigned warehouses remain independent and may include multiple warehouses. Role changes affect subsequent requests and never rewrite historical actors or decisions. Warehouse Staff is the canonical name for the existing Inventory Staff role, not an additional role. Each department records and confirms its own work using personal accounts (D2). Purchasing and delivery personnel may be external business participants but receive no new application role (D3). Survey-reported delegation does not automatically confer software approval rights.
#
	Actor
	Description
	1
	Store Owner
	Approve replenishment, TR, transfer differences, PO remainder closure, OTHER receipts, scoped on-site dispositions, eligible CR exceptions and B1 resolution; plan stocktake and perform Clear Difference; maintain products, UOM/conversions and the warehouse/location catalogue. Distinct requester for Owner decisions. No BF-08 dispatch/variance/closure approval.
	2
	Accountant
	Agree terms with the supplier, then create, edit (DRAFT only), issue and reschedule PO after RR approval; request and execute PO remainder closure; decide supplier custody; verify supplier-return agreements, reconcile ACK/variances and close supplier-return warehouse work via UC-92, ; record independent external Invoice/Credit Note references. No stock posting or operational demand origination.
	3
	Warehouse Staff
	Receive, inspect, put away, pick, post actual dispatch/receipt, relocate, count and isolate within assigned warehouses; confirm customer receipt with mandatory photo POD (UC-129). Source and destination transfer confirmations are independent.
	4
	Sales Staff
	Copy verified external SO, manage linked WIR/OD/net reopened demand; accept/reject ordinary CR after Warehouse inspection, including own CR (C2), but no exception approval. Confirm full external payment after current DELIVERED (UC-130).
	5
	Administrator
	Accounts (lock/unlock with reason), custom roles from the fixed permission catalogue, role and warehouse assignment and technical configuration; no implied business approval or stock-posting rights.

5.3 Background Job Inventory
JOB-01 evaluates committed STOCKTAKE_CLOSED/SALES_ORDER_SAVED events and persists scoped recipient Notifications using a unique event/type/recipient/warehouse dedupKey. Failed or retried processing does not create RR, reset readAt or repeat stock effects. Stocktake warnings are evaluated only after committed closure/unlock, including zero-stock products, and deferred while relevant freezes remain. The current source version accompanies the event. Notification list/unread counts and source opening enforce recipient plus current source/warehouse permissions. Reading does not resolve demand; opening reevaluates current coverage. Optional SMTP delivery uses its own success/failure/attempt tracking; uncertain delivery requires reconciliation.
Finite minimum-stock evaluation occurs on authorized query or committed balance/reservation changes; no automatic PO or duplicate proposal. Monthly stocktake manually planned by Owner; no auto-session job.

II. Use Case Specifications
All active operations require authenticated active user, exact server-side operation/sub-operation and warehouse/record/evidence scope. View does not grant mutation. Source/version is rechecked; typed decisions bind document/version/line/qty and proper actual users; C2 allows Sales own ordinary CR, while Owner approvals require distinct requester. Stock effects commit source/balance/ledger/reservation/status/audit/outbox atomically with stable operation/idempotency keys. Failed confirmation leaves no partial change. Freeze blocks mutation except explicitly authorized clearing.

III. Functional Requirements
The screen layouts and field contracts below support responsive phone use for actual carton/piece/m² quantities, location references and photos. Native mobile applications, offline use and automatic barcode capture are outside scope.
Shared field contract: external provider version is optional if unavailable; internal document/verification version and stable nonblank correlation are retained without inventing a provider revision. ObjectId references, Decimal128 quantities/factors, whole actual pieces for whole-base-unit products, immutable conversion snapshots, UTC Date events displayed in Vietnam time. Required fields are those at the linked UC stage. No invented quantity/factor/deadline/warehouse defaults. Filters optional/nonpersistent; derived balances/approval actor/time/before-after read-only. Positive execution quantity, zero count allowed, blank uncounted, signed nonzero adjustment. Finite states use String enums; version/attempt uses positive Int32, active/found flags Boolean, identifiers/text are trimmed nonblank String where required, evidence uses controlled object-key/hash references.

IV. Non-Functional Requirements
1. External Interfaces
Responsive internal web application including phone. MongoDB runtime requires supported atomic multi-document transactions. SQL Server/T-SQL is reference DDL only. Server-side SMTP API-01 and controlled evidence API-02; no MISA/payment/tax integration. Warehouse outputs derive from committed snapshots and scoped evidence access.
2. Quality Attributes
2.1 Usability
Actual boxes/loose pieces, visible conversion, warehouse/location/lot identity, phone camera/file attachments and per-quantity return/quality review. Failed/missing POD photo retains a draft; retry returns to POD without repicking/PGI. Show copied SO/WIR, PGI, DELIVERED and COMPLETED as distinct milestones. Show physical/available/reserved/custody/transit separately. Distinguish draft, review, physical post, print and external finance status. Errors identify correction without losing valid input or exposing protected data. RR forms show per-line warnings (open RR, incoming PO, sufficient stock) with related documents, require an explanation for warned lines, retain quantity/date/reasons when evaluation changes, and show source links per line. In-app notifications show read/unread per recipient; reading is distinct from resolving a shortage.
2.2 Performance
No signed-off numeric response SLA, concurrency count, volume or availability percentage supplied. Verify scoped list/report behavior on representative store-approved datasets; agree numeric targets before acceptance.
2.3 Security
Active personal accounts, exactly one role per user (five default roles or Administrator-created custom roles built from the fixed permission catalogue) and server-side operation/warehouse/record/evidence scope, including direct requests/exports. Distinct requester for Owner approvals and independent transfer dispatcher/receiver; C2 Sales own ordinary CR acceptance is explicitly allowed, BF-08 Owner approval is absent. Administrator no default business rights. Protect credentials/server secrets, private evidence retrieval and administrative audit; changed access never rewrites past decisions.
2.4 Reliability and Data Integrity
Integrity controls include a shared source guard across POD/FD/CR claims and receipts; atomic GR-FD stock/net reopening/version and replacement OD commitment; B1 balance-neutral holds, current approved plan plus Warehouse execution, delta/RECLASS batch and no early unlock. No Owner supplier-return approval route is in scope.
Atomically commit source/version, batch, movements, balance, reservations, status, audit and outbox. Stable operation/idempotency key and hash prevent duplicate stock effects; changed payload conflicts. Unknown commit resolved by lookup; transient retry uses same identity. Concurrent holds/receipts/returns respect eligible source ceilings. All in-scope quantity/quality/ownership/location changes and backdated corrections coordinate with stocktake activation/freeze; only authorized clearing changes counted balance before unlock. Interrupted session retains freeze/history. Snapshot, count attempts, posted documents/assessments remain immutable; corrections append links. New documents receive _id = documentNo ([type]-[warehouse/department]-[YYMMDD]-[6-digit sequence]) inside the same transaction, retrying on collision; IDs never change after save.
POD/PAY-CFM use stable business-operation IDs/hash, not posting_batch/movement. Atomically commit confirmation/VOIDED correction with SO version/progress, audit/outbox. Competing PODs contend on shared source/SO guards, not merely snapshot sums. PAY-CFM binds current delivered SO version; correction invalidates stale completion conditions.
RR saves re-evaluate advisory warnings inside the transaction and use stable command identities; the shared coverage guard was removed on 11/11/2026 and duplicate purchasing is controlled by confirmed explanations and independent Owner review. Form opening/cancellation has no persistence effect. Post-commit Notification retries preserve existing readAt and deduplicate event/type/recipient/warehouse without creating RR. Output/notification retry never repeats stock posting. SMTP uncertain timeout requires delivery reconciliation, not a claim of exactly-once email. Transit/received/resolved reconcile; custody never silently becomes owned stock.
2.5 Maintainability and Traceability
129 active UCs (UC-11 retired) map to feature/permission/screen/inputs/alternates/exact inventory effect. SC-25–27 are historical screens only. Trace external system/document/line/version→SO→WIR→OD→GI-S→POD→PAY-CFM and PO→GR/transfer→return/disposition/adjustment, with actor/time/evidence. Reconcile closing=opening+ledger delta in consistent dimensions including OPENING; no silent balance initialization/overwrite. Business branches and EX-01–05 define acceptance cases for source validity, permissions, retries and inventory consistency.
