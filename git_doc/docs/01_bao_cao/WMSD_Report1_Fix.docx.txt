Definition and Acronyms
SO = copied external Sales Order; POD = photo Proof of Delivery; PAY-CFM = external payment-receipt confirmation; WIR = Warehouse Issue Request; OD = Outbound Delivery; PGI = Post Goods Issue; TD = Transfer Dispatch; MWTR = destination transfer receipt; FD/GR-FD = failed-delivery case/actual return receipt; RECLASS = total-preserving quality/location posting at B1 closure. Custody is physically held stock owned by another party. Warehouse Staff is the canonical application-role name.

I. Project Overview
1. Project Information
– English name: Warehouse Management System Development
– Vietnamese name: Phát Triển Ứng Dụng Quản Lý Kho
– Project code: WMSD
– Group name: SEP490_G56
– Software type: Responsive Web Application – warehouse operations for one retail store
– Baseline scope: One retail store; the survey reports three or more warehouses with coded areas/racks. Exact warehouse list, addresses and staffing remain to be verified.
2. Project Purpose
Warehouse Management System Development (WMSD) supports the physical warehouse operations of Cửa hàng gạch ốp lát Tân Thương: replenishment needs, purchasing, receiving/inspection, put-away, location movement, inter-warehouse transfer, issue fulfilment, defects, returns and stocktake. Exactly five application roles are Store Owner, Sales Staff, Warehouse Staff, Accountant and Administrator. Each department uses its own personal accounts to record and confirm its work.
The release serves one store and its configured warehouses. Track product, lot, tone, verified actual calibre where available, location, quality, owner/custody, reservations and transit, with versioned BOX/PIECE/m² conversions. Survey-recorded nominal size does not prove actual calibre has already been collected; unknown identity is kept explicit and unavailable for allocation.
WMSD stores an SO manually copied from a verified MISA/paper source, then linked WIR releases. Warehouse reserves, picks, posts actual handover/PGI once, and confirms customer delivery with mandatory photo POD. Fully delivered remaining quantities set DELIVERED; only Sales confirms full money receipt outside WMSD (PAY-CFM) to set COMPLETED. These confirmations do not change inventory or execute financial transactions. D1 reconciliation explains external order-time deduction versus physical WMS dispatch.
MISA and external processes retain commercial Sales Orders, selling prices, internal/tax invoices, refunds, payment, receivables and full accounting. WMSD records source references and external outcomes without creating, calculating, approving or paying a refund. Live MISA synchronization and general-purpose business-policy editing are excluded. Fixed thresholds and compatible-stock suggestions assist decisions without automatically purchasing or posting stock.

III. Existing Solutions


KiotViet and MISA eShop are two existing retail management solutions relevant to the warehouse operations examined in this project. Both provide established capabilities for product management, purchasing, inventory tracking, stocktaking, transfers, and returns.
1. KiotViet
KiotViet Retail supports purchase ordering, multiple receipts against one order, goods tracking by lot/expiry date for enabled items, stock transfers, stock counts and supplier returns. The assessment below compares its documented retail workflows with the specific physical checks used at Tân Thương; it does not assert that the platform is incapable of configuration or extension.
Website / Link
	https://www.kiotviet.vn
	Target Users
	Small and medium retailers and wholesalers using point-of-sale, purchase, inventory and supplier management.
	Core Features
	POS, supplier purchasing, purchase orders, repeated goods receipts, inventory by item, unit handling, optional lot/expiry tracking, warehouse transfers, stock counts, customer and supplier returns, and retail reports.
	Strengths
	The official purchase-order guide explicitly supports multiple goods receipts for one order and the statuses “Nhập một phần” and “Hoàn thành”. The retail transfer guide records dispatched and received quantities, allows a discrepancy note, and updates warehouse stock. Stock counting compares recorded and actual quantities before balancing.
	Scope fit / limits
	Suitable for standard procurement and stock control. The cited workflow descriptions do not establish one mandatory case-specific record joining tile tone, actual calibre, box-to-piece-to-m² conversion, quality inspection, exact storage position and evidence-based handling of exceptions. This is a project fit gap, not a claim that KiotViet lacks lot control or partial receiving.
	GAP for this project
	GAP-01: Multi-receipt PO tracking exists, but Tân Thương still needs per-arrival accepted/rejected/outstanding reconciliation.
GAP-02: Tile tone, actual calibre and package conversion must identify the same physical stock.
GAP-03: Warehouse inspection outcomes must control which received quantities are usable.
GAP-04: Each tile lot requires its real storage bin and a logged location move.
GAP-05: The store needs evidence across transfer dispatch, handover and destination discrepancy resolution.
GAP-06: Actual issue needs the source request, picked lot, bin and physical quantity.
GAP-07: Customer and supplier returns require a warehouse quality decision before release or dispatch.
GAP-08: Paper/Excel counts need a dated snapshot, investigation and controlled adjustment.
GAP-09: Physical warehouse evidence must be connected across the event trail, separate from retail sales records.
	2. MISA eShop
MISA eShop is the retail platform already used by Tân Thương for product, sales and related records. Its official guides cover product variants, lot/expiry tracking, warehouse storage positions, warehouse receipts and transfers, stocktaking, and customer returns. The project therefore addresses the store's remaining end-to-end physical warehouse workflow, not an assumed absence of those standard functions.
The existence, configuration and edition-specific availability of each standard feature must be checked against the store's own MISA eShop account. The project gaps describe operational requirements that are not currently handled consistently in the store's working process; where a vendor guide does not establish a combined control, this is treated as a fit-to-process question rather than a proven product limitation.
Website / Link
	https://www.misaeshop.vn
	Target Users
	Retail SMEs that need sales, stock, tax/invoicing and related operational records on one platform; this is the case store's current retail system.
	Core Features
	Product variants (including colour/size), lot/expiry tracking, multiple warehouses, structured storage positions (including parent position), warehouse receipts, transfers, stocktaking, retail returns, invoicing and reporting.
	Strengths
	MISA eShop documents creation of named warehouse positions and nested locations; the goods-receipt guide includes warehouse and storage-position fields. Product inventory can be viewed by warehouse, attribute and lot where configured. The inventory-count guide records actual counts, differences and causes; transfer-order and return workflows are also documented.
	Scope fit / limits
	MISA eShop is retained for retail and accounting transactions. Although product attributes, lots, storage locations, transfers and stock counts exist, Tân Thương currently also uses paper/separate warehouse records. The cited instructions do not by themselves verify that the installed configuration enforces a single inspection-to-bin-to-picking trail with case-specific proof at every physical checkpoint.
	GAP for this project
	GAP-01: The store must reconcile each supplier delivery and quality-accepted balance with its purchase record.
GAP-02: Standard attributes and lot tracking must be tied to tile-specific tone, actual calibre and unit conversions.
GAP-03: Receipt inspection must split usable, pending, damaged and rejected quantities by delivery.
GAP-04: Existing storage-position functions need consistent deployment and traceable physical relocations at Tân Thương.
GAP-05: Transfer orders need locally recorded dispatch, handover, arrival and discrepancy evidence.
GAP-06: Warehouse issues need actual picked lot, position and issued quantity against their source request.
GAP-07: Physical inspection and quarantine must precede retail return/accounting settlement.
GAP-08: Count cut-off, recount, cause and review are needed beyond the store's current paper/Excel method.
GAP-09: Warehouse proofs and exceptions must form one event history with MISA document references.
	3. Consolidated Gaps and Section Summary
Gap ID
	Operational mismatch with existing solutions and store practice
	Business impact
	GAP-01
	Partial supplier delivery reconciliation: KiotViet already documents partial goods receipts; MISA provides purchase-related receiving. At Tân Thương, delivered, quality-accepted, defective and outstanding quantities are not currently consolidated by supplier confirmation and each arrival.
	A purchase order can appear settled while goods remain to be received or resolved.
	GAP-02
	Tile-specific identity and unit consistency: The platforms support standard item attributes and lot-related inventory. The store needs its lot, tone, actual calibre, packaging specification and box/piece/m² equivalent recorded and validated together when goods are handled.
	A matching item code can conceal a wrong tile batch or inconsistent stock quantity.
	GAP-03
	Inspection-to-usable-stock separation: Retail receipts exist, but the store also checks packaging, tone, calibre and damage manually. Its separate notes do not consistently connect each inspection outcome to accepted, waiting, damaged or rejected quantity.
	Unverified stock can be presented as available for picking.
	GAP-04
	Position-level stock and movement trace: MISA eShop documents hierarchical storage positions; the survey confirms coded areas/racks, while consistent linkage of every move to lot/status and evidence remains the proposed control.
	Staff cannot reliably locate the intended lot or explain a misplaced quantity.
	GAP-05
	Transfer checkpoints and exception ownership: KiotViet documents dispatch/receipt and difference notes; MISA documents transfer orders. The store needs attributable evidence for dispatch, carrier handover and destination receipt, followed by a documented resolution of short or damaged quantities.
	It is difficult to establish where a transfer discrepancy occurred and who must resolve it.
	GAP-06
	Actual warehouse picking and issue: Existing retail sales and stock-issue functions do not replace the store's need to tie a physical issue request to the exact picked lot, location, quality status and issued quantity.
	The posted issue may differ from what was actually handed over.
	GAP-07
	Warehouse treatment of returns: Both products support returns as retail transactions. Tân Thương needs an independent physical check for customer-return quality and supplier-return quarantine, with accepted and rejected quantities recorded before stock disposition.
	Damaged or unresolved returned goods may be mixed with usable stock.
	GAP-08
	Stocktake cut-off and controlled adjustment: Both products support stock counts and variance handling. The survey reports monthly counts, area pauses, recount/cause and approval; durable software snapshot/attempt history and frozen scope through resolution are proposed controls.
	Discrepancies cannot be reconstructed reliably after a stock adjustment.
	GAP-09
	Unified physical warehouse audit trail: MISA eShop provides retail documents and reports, but the store's physical inspection, bins, handovers and exception evidence remain partly in separate records. WMSD will keep source-document references rather than duplicate retail or financial processing.
	An individual quantity cannot be followed across receiving, storage, movement, issue and return.
	Section Summary. KiotViet and MISA eShop already offer established retail and inventory functions. The nine GAPs describe the store's unresolved physical controls and fragmented records. WMSD addresses tile-specific inspection, traceable stock events, exception resolution and count accountability, together with source-linked warehouse issue fulfilment and external-document reconciliation. Financial accounting and sales payment/debt management remain outside WMSD.

BF-01 — Replenishment, purchasing and goods receipt
Main flow: Sales/Warehouse raises uncovered demand; Owner reviews; Accountant creates PO/confirmation; Warehouse records each arrival, inspection and accepted GR, then creates/confirms put-away TO.
Alternate/exception outcomes: Separate accepted ownership, custody and immediate rejection. Rejected goods do not pass through owned GR/put-away. Partial supply and replacements preserve actual source/evidence; no GR0 or double receipt.

BF-02 — Defect detection, isolation and disposition
Main flow: Warehouse records evidence and blocks affected allocation immediately. Outside frozen stocktake, Owner approves scoped scrap/regrade/release; Warehouse executes. Supplier return routes directly to BF-08 without Owner authorization.
Alternate/exception outcomes: B1 records balance-neutral hold/evidence, retaining snapshot and freeze; quality/location posts only in approved UC-93 closure batch, without duplicate UC-66 approval. Scrap deducts actual destruction once; supplier return deducts only at UC-82.

BF-03 — Same-warehouse relocation
Main flow: Warehouse creates/confirms TO-RELOCATE actual quantity and locations in one warehouse, preserving identity/quality/owner/reservations and total. No Owner approval, TR, TD or MWTR.
Alternate/exception outcomes: Different warehouse uses BF-04; frozen/short source blocks confirmation; partial tasks retain or traceably cancel remainder.

BF-04 — Inter-warehouse transfer and discrepancies
Main flow: Owner-approved TR → source TD draft before picking → actual TO confirmation if staging → TD/HO at handover → independent destination MWTR → created/confirmed put-away TO.
Alternate/exception outcomes: Repeat dispatch/receipt for partial trips. A matched TD60 against TR100 leaves unissued40 open; it is not transit. Close only after request remainder, transit, variances and all tasks resolve. Direct customer dispatch from another warehouse uses BF-06 without fictitious receipt.

BF-05 — Stocktake, reconciliation and B1 resolution
Main flow: Owner plans; Warehouse resolves open tasks, atomically freezes/snapshots and counts. Substantiated differences may go directly to explanation/plan; unclear/chosen recount uses Start Recount → Enter Recount → comparison with history.
Alternate/exception outcomes: Record defect hold/evidence immediately without changing balance under freeze. Warehouse proposes/confirms execution, Owner UC-92 approves and UC-93 posts actual nonzero delta plus total-preserving RECLASS/location. Book50/count48GOOD+2broken means RECLASS2/total50; count46GOOD+2broken means delta−2 plus RECLASS2/total48. No ADJ0, false shortage or ordinary freeze bypass. Failure/open holds/tasks prevent unlock.

BF-06 — Copied SO, issue, customer delivery and completion
Main flow: Sales copies verified external SO and creates linked WIR/OD; Warehouse drafts GI-S before picking, confirms actual TO execution and posts PGI at handover; Warehouse photo POD establishes customer receipt/DELIVERED; Sales full external receipt PAY-CFM establishes COMPLETED.
Alternate/exception outcomes: Preserve original SO/WIR, net fulfilment and versioned OD commitments. Valid PGI less valid GR-FD stays within source allowance; effective POD + posted GR-FD + OPEN FD claims ≤ valid PGI per GI line. Partial delivery remains PARTIALLY_DELIVERED; missing photo or early/partial payment cannot complete. Only unexecuted or received-back, not-redelivered quantity may be cancelled; unresolved off-site quantity cannot. GR-FD/changed delivery invalidates stale completion authority.

BF-07 — Post-receipt customer return and failed-delivery intake
Main flow CR: Customer-received POD or verified historical delivery → Sales CR → Warehouse custody/inspection/D6 → Sales STANDARD_ACCEPTANCE for ordinary GOOD/UNUSED, proven-term cases, including own CR; Owner EXCEPTION_APPROVAL only for eligible exceptions with distinct requester → Warehouse GR-CR/put-away or actual handback.
Main flow FD (A1): Valid undelivered GI-S plus failure evidence → Warehouse ARR/INS → actual GR-FD by quality, without D6, fabricated POD, Owner approval or customer custody. Commit receipt/claim consumption and exact original SO/WIR/OD net reopening/version together; Sales then chooses wait/redelivery/cancel received-back remainder.
Alternate/exception outcomes: CR + OPEN CR claims cannot exceed eligible customer-received quantity. FD and POD share source caps, consuming a claim never counts it again with receipt. SO10/PGI10/GR-FD10/redelivery10 retains gross dispatch20/receipt10/net10. Ordinary CR never reopens demand. D6 exclusions block both Sales and Owner; UNKNOWN waits. Reversal checks dependent POD/claims/redelivery; finance remains external.

BF-08 — Supplier return and quantity reconciliation
Main flow: Warehouse proposes/isolates; Accountant verifies source/quantity/agreement; Warehouse posts actual GI-SR or custody release/HO; Accountant records later ACK, reconciles outcomes and closes accounted warehouse quantity through UC-83/107.
Alternate/exception outcomes: No Owner dispatch, variance or closure approval and no UC-106 route/historical BF-02 approval prerequisite. Missing agreement or unresolved disputes keep work open. ACK/closure never deduct again; replacement/returned goods require actual receipt. External Invoice/Credit Note and financial status remain independent. On-site destruction/regrade/release still requires BF-02/B1 authority.

Shared failure handling: enforce role/sub-operation/warehouse/source/version and proper decision_kind on the server. Owner approvals require distinct requester; C2 explicitly permits Sales ordinary acceptance of own inspected CR. Transfer receiver is independent of dispatcher. Failed batches roll back fully; unknown commit requires stable operation-key lookup before retry. Photo/upload/email retry cannot post stock again.

Figure policy: current Mermaid contexts/flows/ERDs and related BF-04 PNG/reduced-ERD HTML exports are updated at baseline 3.1.0. Only draw.io files remain unchanged at their recorded versions and must not override current text; historical diagrams retain their historical labels. External Word/Google Docs require separate publication.
